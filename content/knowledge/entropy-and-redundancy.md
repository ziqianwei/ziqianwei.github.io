## Main idea

Entropy measures uncertainty in a probability distribution. The quantity is largest when the outcomes are distributed uniformly.

## What I want to remember

The useful part is not only the formula, but the comparison: how uncertain is this distribution relative to the most uncertain distribution on the same state space?

This is where redundancy becomes intuitive.
